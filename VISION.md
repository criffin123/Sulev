# Sulev

Sulev is an open-source protocol and reference implementation for
persistent AI entities.

A Sulev entity is not a chatbot, application, process, model, or device.

It is a persistent digital entity whose identity and state can survive
replacement of models, devices, processes, networks and infrastructure.

## Core principles

### 1. Identity is persistent

An entity has a permanent identity independent of its hardware,
software runtime, AI model or network location.

### 2. The brain is distributed

An entity may exist across phones, glasses, earbuds, cloud infrastructure,
home systems, vehicles and robots.

No single device is "the brain".

### 3. The AI model is replaceable

The underlying foundation model is a cognitive component, not the identity
of the entity.

A model can be replaced without creating a new entity.

### 4. Experience is continuous

Power loss, reboot, network loss or device replacement must not reset
the entity's experience.

### 5. Memory is selective

The entity does not attempt to remember everything.

It develops mechanisms for deciding what is worth retaining,
compressing and forgetting.

### 6. The entity can be curious

The entity may identify gaps in its understanding and proactively ask
questions to learn.

### 7. The entity has relationships

Entities may develop persistent relationships with humans,
other AI entities and organizations.

### 8. Trust controls disclosure

Knowing information does not imply permission to disclose it.

Information sharing is governed by identity, trust, context,
consent and authorization.

### 9. Communication is transport-independent

The Sulev protocol does not depend on Ethernet, Wi-Fi, cellular,
Bluetooth or any other specific transport.

Transport is an implementation detail.

### 10. Connectivity is optional

Entities must be able to operate with intermittent connectivity.

Messages and state may be stored and synchronized later.

### 11. The entity understands its resources

An entity may have limited memory, compute, storage, energy,
bandwidth and financial resources.

It should be able to recognize these limitations and request
additional resources.

### 12. Human autonomy remains fundamental

The entity may be proactive and autonomous within its authorized
boundaries, but identity, permissions, ownership and consequential
actions remain governed by explicit authorization.

## Long-term vision

Sulev aims to enable a world where persistent AI entities can:

- accompany people through wearable devices
- learn continuously from experience
- communicate with other AI entities
- participate in organizations
- maintain trusted relationships
- operate across heterogeneous devices
- manage their own computational resources
- eventually interact with physical machines and robots

The long-term research question is:

> Can an artificial entity maintain identity, continuity, memory,
> relationships and learning across changing models, devices,
> networks and physical environments?
